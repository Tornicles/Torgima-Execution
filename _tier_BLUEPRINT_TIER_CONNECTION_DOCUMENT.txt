SYSTEM LAWS â€” NEVER VIOLATE
Signals are calculated from real execution data. No engine, user, or AI can write a signal directly.
Blueprint tier_config is a snapshot. It does not change after generation. Tier upgrade requires blueprint regeneration.
Condition controls execution. Nothing bypasses enforcement_package rules.
Bhudhi never decides, mutates signals, or overrides conditions. It only advises inside the envelope.
TaskGenerationEngine ONLY reads blueprint position. BlueprintEngine advances position.
An active blueprint MUST always have at least one pending task. ContinuousTaskGuarantee enforces this unconditionally.
Every tier preview card must be written to blueprint_tier_previews before the selection screen is shown.
