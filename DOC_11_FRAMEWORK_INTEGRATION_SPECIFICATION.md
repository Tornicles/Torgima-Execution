### 1) `STRATEGYCORE X` — Strategic Direction & Choice Doctrine

- **Framework Identity**
  - **Official name**: `STRATEGYCORE X Strategic Direction & Choice Doctrine`
  - **Primary authority domain**: strategic intent, competitive choices, priority legitimacy, trade-offs, strategic consistency
  - **Controls**: “what winning means”, “where to play”, “how to win”, rejecting initiatives, invalidating priorities, locking/unlocking direction
  - **Does NOT control**: execution methods/processes/budgets/hiring/incentives/operations/innovation mechanics

- **Core Purpose**
  - Strategy = integrated choices constrained by capability, validated by evidence, defended over time.
  - Prevents planning/aspiration/opinion from masquerading as strategy.
  - Guarantees: singular direction, enforced trade-offs, tested assumptions, strategy survives pressure.

- **Authority & Veto Rules**
  - Requires **exactly one declared Strategic Authority** (“If authority is shared… strategy collapses”).
  - StrategyCore’s output is direction and exclusion; execution belongs elsewhere.
  - **Non-negotiables**: exclusions must exist; fit triangle must be coherent; capability reality cannot be ignored; assumptions must be falsifiable with kill conditions.

- **Trigger & Activation Rules**
  - Activate if any: priorities conflict; direction changes frequently; decisions feel reactive; high effort/low clarity; “what actually matters?” appears.

- **Decision & Output Rules**
  - Outputs: winning statement; where-to-play 5-dimension arena (with exclusions); how-to-win (1–2 advantages); fit triangle paragraph; must-have capabilities list (5–8) + gap rating; disablers list + remove/override/accept risk; assumption registry; translation rounds; strategy logic chain + decision-trigger metrics; monthly defense agenda; quarterly re-choice (lock/refine/re-choose).
  - Conflict handling: authority decides; misaligned initiatives rejected immediately.

- **Constraint & Enforcement**
  - **Forbidden**: “everyone” segment; no exclusions; “we’ll figure it out later”; unfalsifiable assumptions; metrics without decision triggers.
  - **Mandatory**: single authority; capability gate; disabler removal; assumption kill conditions; translation cycles.

- **Learning & Adaptation**
  - Assumption registry + monthly defense + quarterly re-choice enforce test→learn→lock/refine/re-choose.

- **Interaction Matrix (high-level, exact-by-doctrine)**
  - Leads: OmniGrowth, SynergyOps, DecisionVelocity (direction + exclusions)
  - Depends on: AdaptiveIntelligence (signals), FinMatrix (capital reality), Governance & Risk (permission boundaries), CustomerValue (value promise reality), TalentSync (capability gaps), KnowledgeFlow (decision memory)
  - Vetoed by: Governance & Risk (risk override), FinMatrix (liquidity/capital freeze)

## Section 2: Framework Authority Hierarchy (who wins, veto rules, dependencies)

### A. Hard veto rules (absolute, non-negotiable)

1. **`GOVERNANCE & RISK X` overrides everything** on: forbidden actions, limit breaches, crisis declaration, containment-first actions.
2. **`FINMATRIX X` overrides everything** on: cash truth, liquidity protection, capital caps/freezes, staged release, shock mode.
3. **`STRATEGYCORE X` overrides all “direction disputes”**: if teams disagree on winning/arena/advantage, StrategyCore re-runs declaration; translation rounds enforce alignment.
4. **`SYNERGYOPS X` overrides execution intake** via WIP Integrity Law: if WIP breached → intake freeze; work cannot bypass board truth.
5. **`ALIGNMENT & ACCOUNTABILITY X` overrides ambiguous ownership**: shared ownership is forbidden; ownership/decision rights must be clarified and enforced.
6. **`CUSTOMERVALUE X` overrides “internal success” claims**: if customer outcomes aren’t achieved or trust breaches repeat → system correction required.

### B. Conflict resolution order (deterministic)

When frameworks conflict, resolve in this exact order:

1) **Governance & Risk**: permitted/forbidden + containment  
2) **FinMatrix**: liquidity + caps + staged release  
3) **CustomerValue**: promise/trust integrity (what must be true for customers)  
4) **StrategyCore**: winning/arena/advantage choices + exclusions  
5) **AdaptiveIntelligence**: whether reality shifted enough to trigger adaptation/rollback  
6) **Alignment & Accountability**: ownership/authority/incentives/escalation structure  
7) **DecisionVelocity**: decision class/deadlines/evidence minimums/bias gates  
8) **SynergyOps**: flow/WIP/capacity protection/constraint removal  
9) **OmniGrowth**: demand/conversion/retention/experiments/scale (must clear gates)  
10) **TalentSync**: capability proof, leadership load tests, succession, capacity protection  
11) **CultureDynamics**: norm enforcement, toxicity containment, inoculation  
12) **KnowledgeFlow**: capture/validate/index/refresh/transfer memory  
13) **Regeneration & Exit**: forced endings/resets/sunsets/exits to reduce decay/complexity  
14) **Clarity Catalyst**: founder-level clarity prerequisite (pre-execution) + drift correction

**Note**: `REGENERATION & EXIT X` can be invoked “out of band” whenever decay triggers exist, but still must respect Governance/FinMatrix constraints.
