435. Identity Modeling Framework
Domain: Extended World, Organization, and AGI Layer
Purpose: Builds evolving identities.
Problem it solves: It solves the problem of identity state being unmanaged, invisible, inconsistent, or disconnected from Bhudhi's larger operating state.
Inputs: world observations; organizational signals; human behavior patterns; strategic goals; simulation history; governance limits.
How it solves the problem: It solves this by modeling the identity state across people, systems, incentives, risks, and long-term alignment constraints.
Operating lifecycle:
1. Capture broad world, organizational, human, or strategic signals.
2. Build a structured model of actors, systems, incentives, and constraints.
3. Simulate interactions across time, scale, and hidden dependencies.
4. Detect identity shifts, social pressure, opportunities, and systemic risk.
5. Generate strategic options and governance-safe interventions.
6. Validate outputs against alignment, reality, and long-term continuity.
7. Route decisions to execution, simulation, memory, or governance.
8. Learn from outcomes and improve cross-domain intelligence.
Output: The framework produces a cross-domain intelligence model with strategic insight, risks, opportunities, and aligned actions. For this framework, the central object is the identity state.
Validation method: Validation checks whether the framework improved model fidelity, strategic usefulness, alignment stability, and whether its output remained aligned with context and rules.
Success metrics: model fidelity; strategic usefulness; alignment stability; risk anticipation; cross-domain transfer.
Failure modes: oversimplified model; hidden incentives; scale mismatch; alignment drift; governance blind spot.
Evolution behavior: It evolves by saving outcomes, comparing predicted versus actual results, adjusting thresholds, improving routing, and retiring weak assumptions.
Bhudhi handoff: Output routes to the next needed layer: validation, memory, simulation, governance, execution, or evolution.
