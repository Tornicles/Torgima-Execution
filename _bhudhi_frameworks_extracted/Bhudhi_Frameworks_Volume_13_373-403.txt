373. Governance Monitoring Framework
Domain: Governance and Control
Purpose: Observes compliance.
Problem it solves: It solves the problem of monitoring record being unmanaged, invisible, inconsistent, or disconnected from Bhudhi's larger operating state.
Inputs: policies; permissions; decisions; audit requirements; risk level; authority model.
How it solves the problem: It solves this by checking the monitoring record against authority, policy, permissions, audit needs, and controlled exceptions.
Operating lifecycle:
1. Receive a decision, policy question, permission request, or compliance event.
2. Identify the governing rules, authority level, and risk class.
3. Check alignment with policy, accountability, audit, and control boundaries.
4. Approve, deny, constrain, or escalate the action.
5. Record the reason, actor, authority, and evidence path.
6. Monitor downstream compliance and policy drift.
7. Update control recommendations when repeated failures appear.
8. Preserve governance history for review and evolution.
Output: The framework produces a governance decision with rule basis, authority trail, constraints, and audit evidence. For this framework, the central object is the monitoring record.
Validation method: Validation checks whether the framework improved policy adherence, audit completeness, unauthorized action rate, and whether its output remained aligned with context and rules.
Success metrics: policy adherence; audit completeness; unauthorized action rate; decision traceability; governance drift.
Failure modes: unclear authority; policy conflict; missing audit trail; over-permissioning; uncontrolled exception.
Evolution behavior: It evolves by saving outcomes, comparing predicted versus actual results, adjusting thresholds, improving routing, and retiring weak assumptions.
Bhudhi handoff: Output routes to the next needed layer: validation, memory, simulation, governance, execution, or evolution.
